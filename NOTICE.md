# What the MIT licence of this repository covers

## These five components, and nothing else

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the code in this repository: the five
components `text-scramble`, `velocity-marquee`, `dot-repel`, `button-01` and
`pixel-ribbon` (engine, stylesheet, snippet, demo page and documentation),
the modules in `esm/` and the scripts in `scripts/`.

It covers nothing else from Moving parts. The rest of the library is
available on [movingparts.supply](https://movingparts.supply/?from=github)
under the licence of the site, which does not allow its components to be
republished: [movingparts.supply/licence/](https://movingparts.supply/licence/?from=github).
A copy of one of these five components taken from the site with an account
is under that licence too; the copy in this repository is under MIT.

The name Moving parts and its mark are not part of the grant.

## What is in this repository under another licence

- **The photographs** in `text-scramble/images/` are by Tom Crew, from
  [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), cropped for the demo. Their origin is in
  `text-scramble/images/sources.json`. They are under the
  [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), not under MIT, and they
  are not in the npm package. Replace them with pictures of your own.

## What is not in this repository

- **GSAP.** `velocity-marquee` and `button-01` play with GSAP 3. It is not
  copied here: the demo pages load it from its CDN, and the npm package
  names it as a peer dependency. GSAP is under
  [its own licence](https://gsap.com/standard-license), which is not MIT.
- **Geist and Geist Mono**, the typefaces of the demos, under the SIL Open
  Font License. The demo pages load them from Google Fonts.

## The words of the demos

The demo pages are set with placeholder copy for a fictional paper company.
It is there to show the effect on real sentences; put your own in its place.
